Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data collection

MindFort does not operate a separate account system or send your app data to servers controlled by the MindFort developer. Profiles, schedules, limit definitions, focus session history, and cross-device focus records are stored privately on your device. When iCloud is available, these supported records can sync privately between devices using the same Apple Account. Detailed Screen Time reports stay on the device that provides them.

Screen Time and usage data

On iPhone and iPad, MindFort uses Apple's Screen Time controls to protect selected apps, app categories, and supported websites during focus sessions and after a daily limit is reached. This requires your explicit permission. Apple represents your selections with protected system identifiers, so MindFort cannot use them to learn which specific apps you chose.

Apple processes detailed app and category usage inside a private Screen Time report on your device. MindFort can display the durations and pickup counts from that report without copying app- or category-level details into its iCloud sync data.

MindFort can also keep a local daily estimate of how often its block screen appeared so it can show your focus statistics. The system may count one blocked attempt more than once, so these numbers are estimates. They stay on that device and are not sent to the developer for analytics.

Mac app and website protection

On Mac, protection can close the native apps you select during a focus session or after a daily limit is reached. Website protection uses built-in macOS protection and, in supported browsers, Automation access to identify the current page and show MindFort's local block page. These selections and checks are used only to provide protection on your Mac and are not sent to the MindFort developer.

Local app data

Features that work together on one device—including the block screen, activity monitoring, widgets, and Live Activities—share the information they need in protected local storage. This local information is separate from the supported records that may sync through iCloud.

Analytics and tracking

MindFort does not include third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising. The on-device Screen Time measurements and local block-screen estimates described above are used only to show you personal insights. They are not sent to the MindFort developer or any developer-operated analytics service.

Notifications

Local notifications are used for session starts and ends, a reminder shortly before a session ends, active open-ended focus sessions, cross-device focus requests, daily-limit status, and the optional weekly focus report. They are processed on-device and are not sent through a developer-operated notification service.

Third-party services

MindFort contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or developer-operated cloud service. It uses Apple's built-in services, including iCloud for optional private sync. Apple's privacy terms apply to data processed by Apple services.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting MindFort removes its local app data from that device. Data already synced to iCloud is not automatically deleted when you delete the app; it may remain in iCloud and sync again after reinstalling MindFort or using another signed-in device. You can manage or delete MindFort's iCloud data from your Apple Account's iCloud storage settings. Menu names may vary by operating-system version.

Children's privacy

MindFort is designed for personal adult use. It requests individual Screen Time permission and does not provide child or parental controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app update notes and on this page.
