Now on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Get your focus back.

MindFort blocks distracting apps and websites, limits screen time, and keeps focus in sync across your Apple devices. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Download on theApp Store Download on theMac App Store

One download for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Requires iOS 17, iPadOS 17, or macOS 15 or later. Need help?

Block what breaks your flow

Pick what pulls you away, such as social media, games, video, or news, then start a focus session and let MindFort hold the line.

Apps & websites

Build reusable profiles for work, study, social media, and games. On iPhone and iPad, protection uses Apple's Screen Time. On Mac, MindFort closes selected apps and blocks selected websites in supported browsers.

Choose how strict

Easy stops whenever you choose. Medium adds a 30-second pause. Hard locks you in, with 3 Emergency Passes per device each day. Easy and Medium include 3 breaks a day.

Make focus automatic

Recurring schedules for work hours, study blocks, mornings, and evenings. Add daily time limits, timed access on iPhone and iPad, or daily access on Mac.

Across your devices

Start on this device, or start here and try your other devices too. Stop on one device or on every active device. Everything syncs privately with iCloud.

See your progress

Actual Screen Time by day, week, month, and year on iPhone and iPad, plus focus history, streaks, and an optional weekly focus report.

Private by design

No MindFort account. No ads and no cross-app tracking. Your data syncs only through your own Apple Account.

A native Mac app, too

MindFort on Mac lives in your menu bar, closes the apps you pick, and blocks websites while you focus. Start a session on your iPhone and bring it to your Mac in one click.
